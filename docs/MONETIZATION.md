# Monetization

## Principle
Do not primarily sell watching videos. Sell the ability to **create new worldlines / unexplored reality**.

Existing/cached content has low marginal generation cost; genuinely new StoryNode/video generation has real cost.

> 消费发生在“改变宇宙”的瞬间。

## Credits first, subscription second
Initial abstraction: Dream Credits / product credits (final naming TBD).

Illustrative only, not final pricing:
- cached branch continuation: 0
- new text node: 1
- new 5s video: 5
- custom action + new video: 6
- premium quality/HD: 10

Actual prices must be calibrated against provider costs and gross margin.

## Potential paid capabilities
- generation of unexplored video branches
- more Custom Actions
- premium generation profiles (HD/longer/continuity)
- private timelines
- deeper timelines
- priority generation
- later: create/private/commercial Worlds and creator features

## Subscription later
Free Explorer:
- public-world browsing
- public/shared timeline viewing
- small generation allowance
- base quality

Dreamer Pro / final name TBD:
- monthly credits
- lower incremental generation price
- premium generation profiles
- more custom actions
- private timelines
- priority queue

Do not promise unlimited AI video because costs are variable.

## Future creator economy
Potential long-term revenue:
- user-created Worlds
- AI World Factory (generate world config/characters/locations/events/rules)
- reference asset uploads
- paid Worlds
- creator revenue share
- platform take rate
- commercial/private Worlds

## Cost accounting
Track provider cost, cache-hit rate, credits charged, videos per timeline, cost per world/session, and paid-user gross margin through UsageEvent/ledger data.
